Time In Place – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · App package: com.trunglabs.timeinplace · Developer: TrungLabs

Time In Place is an Android app that automatically records the places you stay at and for how long, and summarises your days and weeks. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your data stays on your phone; the only copy that can leave it is the backup you choose to keep in your own Google Drive.

Summary

  • We (the developer) run no server that receives your location history, photos, notes or expenses.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no sale or sharing of your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Looking up place names online and signing in to Google for backup are optional; the app works without them.

2. Data that leaves your phone

2.1 Place-name lookup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Look up place names online", the coordinates of a place where you just stayed (and nothing else) are sent to a map data provider to find nearby place names: Microsoft Azure Maps, or, if that service is unavailable or its monthly allowance for your device is used up, OpenStreetMap services (Overpass and Nominatim). Requests are made only once per new visit and results are cached on your phone. When using OpenStreetMap services while you are signed in with Google, your Google email address is added to the request's User-Agent as a contact address, as that service's usage policy asks. If you leave the lookup off, no coordinates are sent for this purpose.

See Microsoft's privacy statement and the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

2.2 Maps display (Google Maps)

When a map is shown, the Google Maps SDK loads map tiles from Google. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and device information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app does not send your visit history to Google Maps.

2.3 Backup to Google Drive (optional)

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for backup. If you sign in and turn backup on, the app stores a backup file (and copies of photos you attached to visits) in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. The app requests only the drive.appdata permission, so it cannot see any other file in your Drive. The backup includes: places, visits, notes, expenses, goals, privacy rules, trips, most settings, the list of removed photos, cached place lookups, and photos attached to visits. It does not include raw location points, your account details, the online-lookup choice or pause state. Backups can be sent only over Wi-Fi if you choose. We, the developer, cannot access this backup.

You can remove the backup at any time from Settings → Google Drive backup → "Delete backup on Drive".

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for profiling.
  • We do not upload your location history, photos, notes or expenses to any server operated by the developer.
  • We do not read notifications of apps you have not chosen, and we do not store the content of notifications.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Location: change or revoke location, background location and activity recognition in Android Settings → Apps → Time In Place → Permissions. The app then records only while open, or not at all.
  • Pause or restrict recording: use the quick pause or time/place privacy rules in Settings; no location is stored during those periods.
  • Photos and notifications: both are opt-in and can be turned off at any time in Settings; notification access can also be revoked in Android Settings.
  • Online lookup and backup: optional and can be turned off; signing out stops backup and keeps data on your phone.
  • Access and deletion: all your data is inside the app and can be deleted there (delete a visit's note, expenses or photos, delete a place). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings removes everything stored on the phone.

5. Deleting your data

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, deleting it is done on your side:

  1. On your phone: uninstall Time In Place, or Android Settings → Apps → Time In Place → Storage → Clear storage.
  2. In Google Drive (only if you used backup): open Settings → Google Drive backup → "Delete backup on Drive" in the app before uninstalling. You can also remove the app's hidden data at Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps and revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Data retention

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it or remove the app. Cached place lookups expire after a limited time. Dismissed notification-based expenses are removed automatically after about 30 days. A Google Drive backup stays in your Drive until you delete it.

7. Children

Time In Place is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Network requests use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and updated.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's permission and settings screens.
