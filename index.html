Place Diary: Time & Money – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Android package: com.trunglabs.timeinplace · Developer: TrungLabs

Place Diary helps you keep a private journal of places, time and spending. This policy explains the data the app handles, why it handles it, where it goes, and the controls available to you.

At a glance

  • Your location history, journal, notes, expenses, photos and AI chat history are stored on your device by default.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your location history, journal, notes, expenses or attached photos.
  • Optional features can send data to Google Drive, Google Maps, Huawei Petal Maps, Google Firebase/Gemini, or another app or person you choose. The relevant sections below explain exactly what is sent.
  • There are no ads. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or cross-app advertising profiling.

1. Data stored on your device

Location samples and derived visits are kept in the app's local database. Background location lets the journal work when the app is not open. Raw location points are never put in the optional Drive backup.

2. When data leaves your device

2.1 Place lookup with Huawei Petal Maps

When the app suggests a name for a stay or nearby place, it sends the coordinates of that stay to Huawei Petal Maps. This is the app's only online place-lookup provider. It does not send your account email, journal notes or other history with that request. Results are cached on your device for about 30 days; lookup stops when the applicable quota is exhausted or the service is not configured. See Huawei's Privacy Policy.

2.2 Map display with Google Maps

The app uses the Google Maps SDK to display maps. When a map is shown, Google receives the map requests and technical information such as your IP address, device information and map area, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app does not upload your journal, place names, notes or transaction data to Google Maps.

2.3 Optional Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google and enable backup, the app uses the restricted drive.appdata scope to store files only in your Google Drive app-data folder. The developer cannot access that folder. Backups can include places, visits, tracking segments, notes, expenses (except dismissed suggestions), goals, budgets, recurring payments, debts, people and their small avatar copies, groups, privacy rules, trips, movement markers, attached photo copies, selected settings, and cached place lookups. They do not include raw GPS points, notification account/balance checking data, your Google credentials, AI chat history, or AI usage counters. You can choose Wi-Fi-only photo backup, sign out to stop backup, or delete the backup from the app.

2.4 Optional QR sharing and system sharing

QR sharing is offline. The selected data is compressed and encrypted on your device using a PIN you set, then encoded in QR codes or a GIF. It leaves the device only when you choose to share it through Android's share sheet. Anyone with both the shared QR/GIF and the PIN can read it, so share the PIN only with the intended recipient. We do not receive or host this data.

2.5 Firebase crash reporting and usage analytics

The release app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and unexpected-error reports and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature use. These services may receive a randomly generated app-instance identifier, device model, Android and app version, language, technical error/stack-trace information, app mode and coarse, predefined feature events. Analytics events use fixed names and coarse values or ranges; automatic screen reporting, advertising ID collection, SSAID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. We do not set a user ID.

These reports and analytics do not include coordinates, addresses, place names, visit history, money amounts, notification text, photos, file names, QR PINs, email addresses, or text you type. Crash reporting and analytics are disabled in the app's trial/demo mode. Firebase processes this data for us; see Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

2.6 Optional Pro AI features (Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic)

Pro users can use two AI features when Firebase AI Logic is available: receipt scanning and Ask AI. The app sends these requests to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic, protected by Firebase App Check. AI is not used for payment-notification reading, transaction sorting, place suggestions, or goal suggestions.

  • Receipt scanning: the app sends a reduced-size photo of the receipt to identify its total, currency, merchant, category, and date/time. If the AI result is unavailable or invalid, the app falls back to local processing.
  • Ask AI: the app sends the question you type, up to four preceding chat turns, a description of app features, and locally calculated summaries such as time by place category, totals for spending/received money by category and currency, budget progress, goals, recurring payments and debt totals. It does not send coordinates, addresses, place names, people's names, transaction notes, notification text, or your photo library.

AI-generated actions are only proposals: no expense, budget or goal is saved unless you confirm it. AI chat history stays on your device and is not included in Drive backup. Do not enter personal information in an AI question unless you want it included in that request. See the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

2.7 Google Play purchases

Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment information. The app asks Google Play only for purchase status needed to unlock Pro on the device; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

2.8 Import, export and on-device assistants

When you choose to import a Google Timeline export, the app reads the selected file locally and imports stays, not raw location points; it does not upload the file. CSV exports are written to a folder you choose through Android's system picker and are not sent by the app. On supported Android versions, a system assistant may invoke an app action such as adding an expense or asking for a total; the action runs locally in the app. Any handling by that assistant is governed by its own policy.

3. Permissions and your choices

  • Location: revoke precise, approximate, or background location in Android Settings. Without the needed permission, recording is limited or stops.
  • Privacy controls: pause recording or create time- and place-based privacy rules. During a private period, the app does not store location points, activity markers, or visits.
  • Activity, photos, camera and notifications: these optional permissions/features can be denied, revoked, or disabled in Android Settings and the app's settings. Camera access is used only while a QR scan or photo-taking screen is open.
  • Backup: Google sign-in and Drive backup are optional. Signing out stops future backup; delete the remote backup from the backup settings if you no longer want it in Drive.
  • AI and sharing: use them only when you choose. Ending Pro or an unavailable AI service prevents further AI requests.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stored by the app remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. You can delete individual places, visits, notes, transactions, attached photos, QR shares and AI chat history in the relevant features. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes local app data.

If you enabled Drive backup, delete it from Settings → Google Drive backup → Delete backup on Drive before uninstalling, or remove the app's hidden data in Google Drive settings. Backups remain in your Drive until removed. Firebase retention is governed by the Firebase service configuration and Google policies.

5. Security

Local data is kept in Android app-private storage. Received QR shares are encrypted with Android Keystore. Network connections use HTTPS. No system can be perfectly secure; keep your device locked and up to date.

6. Children

Place Diary is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
